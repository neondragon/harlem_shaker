Drag the button to your bookmarks bar. Then click it on any page.

No bookmarks bar? ⌘⇧B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B elsewhere.

Or click it to try it right here. Sound on.

On a phone, bookmarklets are fiddly; it's best on a computer. You can still tap it to try it here.

Step by step

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (⌘⇧B or Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the red button onto it. It becomes a bookmark called “🕺 Harlem Shake”.
  3. Open any page with a bit going on, and click the bookmark.

What happens

One thing starts shaking. Then the drop hits and everything joins in.

{{SIZE}}, song included.

Why it broke

Moovweb's original loaded its song and stylesheet from an S3 bucket that no longer exists. Many sites' security policies would block those loads anyway.

What changed

The song is inlined and plays through Web Audio, and the dancing runs on the Web Animations API, so it makes no network requests and needs no stylesheet. It also finds the site's logo to lead the dance.
